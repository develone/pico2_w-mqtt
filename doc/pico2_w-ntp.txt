The Raspberry Pi Pico 2, like its predecessor, does not have a built-in real-time clock (RTC) 
for timekeeping when powered off. Users typically rely on external RTC modules or network 
time synchronization (NTP) to obtain accurate time and date information. 
#!/bin/bash
rm -rf build-rp2350 
mkdir build-rp2350
cd build-rp2350
cmake .. -DPICO_BOARD=pico2_w -DPICO_PLATFORM=rp2350 -DWIFI_SSID="YOUR-WIFI-SSID" -DWIFI_PASSWORD="YOUR-WIFI-PASSWORD"
make
devel@pi5-90:~ $ minicom myusb0
